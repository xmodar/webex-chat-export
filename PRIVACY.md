# Privacy policy

_Webex Chat Export · last updated 2026-10-30_

Webex Chat Export collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

- **What it reads:** the chat messages, sender names, times, reactions and your
  own display name, as shown in a Webex meeting open in your browser on
  `*.webex.com`, and the meeting's title. It reads nothing on any other site.
- **What it stores:** the chat of each Webex meeting you join in the browser,
  saved as the meeting goes in the extension's local storage on your computer,
  so you can export it later. Saved chats stay until you delete them on the
  extension's Saved chats page, and are deleted when you remove the
  extension. They never leave your computer.
- **Where the data goes:** only into the files you export, saved where you
  choose.
- **Settings:** your export options (format, name override, time zone, sheet
  direction) are kept in the same local storage.
- **No network requests:** the extension makes none of its own. It has no
  analytics, tracking, ads or remote code.
- **No sharing:** nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

The source code is public at https://github.com/xmodar/webex-chat-export.
Questions: open an issue there.
